Blockventure Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Blockventure is an offline-first single-player game. Its Android release can back up gameplay progress to Google Play Games after a player signs in, and may offer an optional rewarded ad after a completed result screen. Core play, progress, and retries never require watching an ad.

Publisher and contact

Blockventure is published by Jiradech Tanawaranan. The Google Play developer name is LumaKeep Games. For privacy questions or support, contact j.onsuratum@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, settings, mastery, missions, inventory, and an active-run checkpoint locally on your device so that the game can work offline. When you sign in to Google Play Games, the game also backs up that game-profile data to Google Play Games Saved Games. We do not operate a separate game-account system.

You may optionally enable Local Diagnostics in Settings > Privacy. If enabled, the game keeps a maximum of 200 compact diagnostic records on your device, such as feature events, integer counters, error severity, and one-way error hashes. These records remain on your device, are not uploaded, and do not include your name, email address, advertising identifier, location, contacts, message text, or account information.

Optional rewarded advertising

When you explicitly choose an offered post-results rewarded ad and privacy requirements allow an ad request, Blockventure uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). We do not show banner ads, forced interstitials, ads during battle, ads before a retry, or ads in exchange for combat power.

Google's Google Play data disclosure documentation states that Google Mobile Ads automatically collects and shares the device IP address; product-interaction data such as app launches, taps, and video views; diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy use; and device/account identifiers including Android Advertising ID and App Set ID (and, where applicable, identifiers associated with a signed-in device account). Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that this SDK data is encrypted in transit with TLS.

We do not send your name, email address, contacts, messages, precise location, payment information, or game-save contents to Google for advertising. We do not add third-party mediation networks or share custom audience data. Ad delivery can be personalized, non-personalized, or limited according to your region, consent choice, and Google's ad-serving controls.

Privacy choices

At app launch, UMP checks whether a privacy message is required and presents it when needed. Where Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, the game shows an AD PRIVACY button. You can use it to review or change your choices. This control appears only when it is required for your region or consent state.

For more information about Google's handling of data in partner apps, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services.

Google Play Games cloud saves

Google Play Games sign-in uses your existing Google Play Games account. When you choose to sign in, Google Play Games processes the identifiers and basic profile needed to sign in, plus the game-profile data that the game sends to Saved Games, such as progression and owned in-game rewards. The game uses these data only for sign-in and cloud backup. Data is encrypted in transit.

The game keeps a local save first. If Google Play Games is unavailable or sign-in is not completed, gameplay and local saving continue. If both a device and cloud copy contain meaningful progress, the game asks you which copy to keep; it does not silently overwrite one with the other.

Data sharing, sales, and tracking

Outside Google Play Games cloud backup and optional Google advertising described above, Blockventure has no separate account system, remote configuration, purchase SDK, or first-party analytics/crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal information.

Permissions

The game uses Internet access only for optional advertising and privacy messaging, and may use device vibration for optional haptic feedback. You can disable haptics in Settings. The game does not use a foreground service or request wake-lock access. It does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, media, phone, SMS, calendar, or nearby devices.

Your choices and deletion

You can view or delete Local Diagnostics in Settings > Privacy. You can erase game data in Settings > About > Delete Local Data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app also removes locally stored game data, subject to your device and operating-system behavior. Deleting local data does not delete a Google Play Games Saved Games snapshot; Google Play Games data can be managed through your Google account.

Audience and children

Blockventure's ad-supported launch is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not designed for children. It has no user-generated content, chat, or social features. We do not knowingly direct personalized advertising to children. Any future launch aimed at younger players will require a separate age-treatment, consent, and Google Play policy review before release.

Changes to this policy

If we add a new advertising partner, analytics, purchases, accounts, or any other networked service, we will update this policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures before distributing that version.

สรุปภาษาไทย

Blockventure เป็นเกมเล่นคนเดียวแบบออฟไลน์เป็นหลัก รุ่น Android ที่มีโฆษณาอาจเสนอ Rewarded Ad แบบกดเลือกเองหลังหน้าผลการเล่นเท่านั้น การเล่นหลัก การเก็บความคืบหน้า และการเริ่มใหม่ไม่ต้องดูโฆษณา

เมื่อผู้เล่นเลือกดูโฆษณาและการตั้งค่าความเป็นส่วนตัวอนุญาต ระบบ Google Mobile Ads อาจประมวลผล IP ข้อมูลการใช้งาน ข้อมูลวินิจฉัย และตัวระบุอุปกรณ์เพื่อโฆษณา การวิเคราะห์ และป้องกันการทุจริต ระบบ UMP จะแสดงตัวเลือกความยินยอมเมื่อจำเป็น และปุ่ม AD PRIVACY จะปรากฏเมื่อผู้เล่นต้องสามารถแก้ไขตัวเลือกได้

ข้อมูลความคืบหน้าและการตั้งค่าถูกเก็บไว้ในเครื่องเป็นหลัก เมื่อผู้เล่นลงชื่อเข้าใช้ Google Play Games เกมจะสำรองโปรไฟล์ ความคืบหน้า และรางวัลในเกมไปยัง Saved Games ผ่านการเข้ารหัสระหว่างส่งข้อมูล หากไม่มีการลงชื่อเข้าใช้ การเล่นและการบันทึกในเครื่องยังใช้งานได้ตามปกติ

ผู้เล่นสามารถลบข้อมูลภายในเกมได้ที่ Settings > About > Delete Local Data และลบข้อมูลวินิจฉัยใน Settings > Privacy การลบข้อมูลในเครื่องจะไม่ลบ cloud save ของ Google Play Games
