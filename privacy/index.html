Blockventure Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Blockventure is an offline-first single-player game. The current release does not use advertising, in-app purchases, accounts, cloud saves, analytics, or other network services.

Publisher and contact

Blockventure is published by Jiradech Tanawaranan. The Google Play developer name is LumaKeep Games. For privacy questions or support, contact j.onsuratum@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, settings, mastery, missions, inventory, and an active-run checkpoint locally on your device so that the game can work offline. This information is not sent to us or to third parties.

You may optionally enable Local Diagnostics in Settings > Privacy. If enabled, the game keeps a maximum of 200 compact diagnostic records on your device, such as feature events, integer counters, error severity, and one-way error hashes. These records remain on your device, are not uploaded, and do not include your name, email address, advertising identifier, location, contacts, message text, or account information.

Data sharing, sales, and tracking

We do not collect, share, sell, or transmit personal information from the current release. The game has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, social service, remote configuration, cloud-save service, or payment SDK.

Permissions

The game may use device vibration for optional haptic feedback. You can disable haptics in Settings. The current release does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, media, phone, SMS, calendar, or nearby devices.

Your choices and deletion

You can view or delete Local Diagnostics in Settings > Privacy. You can erase game data in Settings > About > Delete Local Data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app also removes locally stored game data, subject to your device and operating-system behavior.

Children

Blockventure is designed for teen and adult strategy-game players and is not designed for children under 13. The current release has no advertising, purchases, user-generated content, chat, or social features.

Changes to this policy

If we add advertising, analytics, purchases, cloud saves, accounts, or any other networked service, we will update this policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures before distributing that version.
